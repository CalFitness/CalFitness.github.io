Features/Progress and insight

Exercise history

Open an exercise and you get its whole history: every session, every set, your records, and the trend in the weight you are moving for it.

Sessions, not just a chart

The chart tells you the direction; the session list tells you what happened. Both are there, because a plateau usually turns out to have an explanation visible in the sets.

Notes stay attached

Anything you noted about an exercise — a cue, a setup, a pin position — is on the exercise, so it is there next time rather than in a notes app.

Questions

Does it include warm-ups?
They are recorded but kept out of the working-set numbers and records.

Try it

CalFitness is free to start. Exercise history is in the app from the day you install it.
