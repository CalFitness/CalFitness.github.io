Features/Progress and insight

The home rail

The home screen shows a rail of insight cards, and every card has to qualify to appear. If nothing about today is worth saying, the rail is short — which is a correct outcome, not an empty state to be filled.

Open it in the app

Earned, not scheduled

Each card has a predicate: a condition in your data that makes it worth showing. No condition, no card. Nothing appears on a rotation just to keep the screen busy.

Every card opens up

A card is a headline. Tapping it opens the full reasoning — what was measured, over what period, and how confident it is. Nothing on the rail is a claim you cannot inspect.

Questions

Why is my home screen empty some days?
Because there was nothing worth interrupting you about. A quiet rail means the app had no finding, not that it failed to compute one.
