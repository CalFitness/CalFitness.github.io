AI workout coach

The in-app coach builds routines and programs from a conversation: your goal, your equipment, how many days you can train, what you are coming back from. The result is a real routine, editable like any other.

Open it in the app

It uses the real catalogue

Exercises come from the actual library with their real equipment requirements, so it does not prescribe a machine you do not have or invent a movement that does not exist.

A draft, not a decree

What it produces lands as an ordinary routine. Change it, delete half of it, keep one day of it — nothing about it is locked because it was generated.

Questions

Does it know my history?
It can use your training summary to pitch the volume and loads sensibly, rather than starting from scratch every time.
